Topper-Recommended Literature

Standard Reference Books for UPSC CSE

Do not hoard 10 books per subject. Master 1 standard book per subject and revise it 5+ times alongside active recall testing.

Indian Polity & Governance

Prelims GS-1 & Mains GS-2

Non-Negotiable (Must Read)

Indian Polity (Latest Edition)

By M. Laxmikanth

The indispensable standard text for constitutional articles, fundamental rights, parliament, and statutory bodies.

Mains GS-2 Focus

Governance in India

By M. Laxmikanth

Covers civil services accountability, citizen charters, RTI, and regulatory frameworks.

Selective Reference

Introduction to the Constitution of India

By D.D. Basu

In-depth constitutional philosophy and legal precedents for high-scoring Mains answers.

Modern Indian History

Prelims GS-1 & Mains GS-1

Non-Negotiable (Must Read)

A Brief History of Modern India

By Rajiv Ahir (Spectrum Publications)

Chronological summary from the advent of Europeans to post-independence events. Essential for Prelims MCQs.

Mains GS-1 Context

India's Struggle for Independence

By Bipan Chandra

Builds narrative depth, ideological currents, and peasant/tribal movements.

Selective Reading

India Since Independence

By Bipan Chandra

Covers linguistic reorganization, non-alignment foreign policy, and planned economic history.

Ancient, Medieval & Art and Culture

Prelims GS-1 & Mains GS-1

Primary Reference

Indian Art and Culture

By Nitin Singhania

Visual architecture guide, classical dances, music, paintings, UNESCO sites, and literature.

Non-Negotiable (Must Read)

An Introduction to Indian Art (Class 11 NCERT)

By NCERT

Direct source of ancient cave art, Mauryan pillars, and Dravidian/Nagara temple architecture questions.

High Priority

Ancient and Medieval India

By Poonam Dalal Dahiya / R.S. Sharma

Harappan sites, Vedic literature, Gupta Golden age, and Chola/Vijayanagara administrative terms.

Geography & Cartography

Prelims GS-1 & Mains GS-1

Non-Negotiable (Part 1)

Certificate Physical and Human Geography

By G.C. Leong

Master climate zones, weather systems, landforms, volcanism, and geomorphology.

Non-Negotiable (Must Read)

Class 11 & 12 Geography NCERTs (4 Books)

By NCERT

Physical geography fundamentals, Indian drainage, soils, minerals, and demographic trends.

Mandatory Map Tool

Orient BlackSwan School Atlas / Oxford Student Atlas

By Orient BlackSwan / Oxford

Essential for daily map-pointing drills (Rivers, Straits, Biosphere Reserves, Bordering Seas).

Indian Economy

Prelims GS-1 & Mains GS-3

Primary Reference

Indian Economy

By Sanjiv Verma / Nitin Singhania / Ramesh Singh

Macroeconomics, banking regulations, inflation, agriculture supply chains, and external trade.

Non-Negotiable (Every Year)

Annual Economic Survey & Union Budget

By Ministry of Finance, Govt. of India

Official data, government schemes, sector growth figures, and policy roadmaps.

Environment, Ecology & Biodiversity

Prelims GS-1 & Mains GS-3

Primary Reference

Environment

By Shankar IAS Academy / PMF IAS

Ramsar wetlands, National Parks, wildlife protection acts, UNFCCC conventions, and carbon mechanisms.

Must Read

Class 12 Biology (Ecology Chapters 13–16)

By NCERT

Organisms and populations, ecosystem trophic levels, biodiversity hotspots, and environmental issues.

Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude

Mains GS-4

Primary Terminology Guide

Lexicon for Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude

By Chronicle Books

Defines foundational ethical terms, moral thinkers, administrative dilemmas, and case studies.

Case Study Reference

Ethics, Integrity and Aptitude

By G. Subba Rao & P.N. Roy Chowdhury

Structured philosophical frameworks and applied ethical decision-making models.

Official Recommended Reading

2nd ARC 4th Report: Ethics in Governance

By Government of India

Authentic recommendations on combating administrative corruption and institutional integrity.

Internal Security & Disaster Management

Mains GS-3

Primary Reference

Challenges to Internal Security of India

By Ashok Kumar (IPS) & Vipul Anekant

Border management, cyber warfare, terrorism financing, left-wing extremism, and money laundering.

Mains Answer Enabler

NDMA Guidelines & 2nd ARC Disaster Management Report

By National Disaster Management Authority

Institutional disaster mitigation plans, early warning systems, and post-crisis rehabilitation.

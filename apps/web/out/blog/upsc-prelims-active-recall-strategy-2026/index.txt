<li class="ml-4 list-disc text-slate-300 mb-1"><strong class="font-bold text-white">Factual Memory Slip</strong>: You confused a treaty year, national park state, or constitutional amendment number.</li>
<li class="ml-4 list-disc text-slate-300 mb-1"><strong class="font-bold text-white">Misreading Slip</strong>: You overlooked words like <em class="italic text-slate-200">"Incorrect"</em>, <em class="italic text-slate-200">"Not"</em>, or <em class="italic text-slate-200">"Always"</em>.</li></p><p class="text-slate-300 text-base sm:text-lg leading-relaxed mb-4"><h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-indigo-300 mt-6 mb-3 tracking-tight">3.
